OpenKOS is an open-source, self-hosted property management platform for boarding houses, apartments, and rental properties.

It covers the rental lifecycle in one workspace:

- **Properties and units**
- **Tenants and leases**
- **Invoices and payments**
- **Rent reminders**
- **Maintenance**
- **Staff access and permissions**
- **Plugin-based integrations**

## Choose your next step

- **New to OpenKOS?** Read [About OpenKOS](/about) to understand the product and its current scope.
- **Installing it locally?** Follow [Installation](/installation) for a source checkout or a published release image.
- **Running a rental operation?** Start with [For end users](/index), then follow the [Quickstart](/quickstart).
