This section is for people who use OpenKOS to get work done. You do not need to know Laravel or the internal codebase.

## The basic OpenKOS workflow

Most rental operations follow this path:

1. Create a property and its units.
2. Add a tenant and assign a unit.
3. Create a lease with its billing schedule.
4. Review invoices and record or verify payments.
5. Handle maintenance requests and keep tenants informed.

OpenKOS keeps the lease at the center of this workflow. Billing is organized around invoices, and payment status is based on confirmed payments.

## Who can use OpenKOS?

- Owners configure the installation, properties, roles, and integrations.
- Admins and staff handle the day-to-day operational work allowed by their permissions.
- Tenants use the portal to understand their lease, review invoices, submit payments or payment proof, and report maintenance issues.
