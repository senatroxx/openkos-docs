OpenKOS organizes rental billing around invoices. The normal chain is:

**Lease → Invoice → Payment → Payment proof**

## Invoice lifecycle

Invoices are generated for active leases and can be:

- **Pending** when no confirmed payment has been recorded.
- **Partial** when a confirmed payment covers only part of the amount.
- **Paid** when the outstanding balance reaches zero.

Invoice status is recalculated from confirmed payments. A rejected payment can move an invoice back to an outstanding state.

## Record a manual payment

1. Open the lease or billing queue.
2. Select the invoice being paid.
3. Enter the amount and payment details.
4. Attach proof when the installation uses manual verification.
5. Save the payment.
6. Verify the payment after checking the proof.

Partial payments are supported. OpenKOS rejects overpayments and prevents a second payment from settling an invoice that is already paid.

## Online payments

When a payment gateway is installed and configured, tenants can start checkout from an invoice. The payment remains pending until OpenKOS receives a trusted provider result.

Do not treat a browser redirect as payment confirmation. Confirmation comes from a provider webhook or a successful provider status lookup.

## Pending gateway attempts

Staff can open an invoice's **Gateway attempts** section to review:

- OpenKOS and provider references.
- Attempt status and expiry.
- Safe failure or anomaly messages.

Use **Recheck status** when a provider reference exists. The reconciliation job also checks eligible pending attempts periodically.

<Warning>
  Never manually mark an invoice paid just because a tenant says checkout succeeded. Check the payment attempt or provider confirmation first.
</Warning>
