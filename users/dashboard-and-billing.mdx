The dashboard is the operational starting point for owners, admins, and staff. It puts the next actions ahead of background information.

## Dashboard overview

The dashboard combines four useful views:

- **Today's Attention** — overdue invoices, invoices due today, open maintenance, leases ending soon, and payments waiting for review.
- **Business Health** — revenue this month, monthly potential, outstanding balance, and collection rate.
- **Property Overview** — occupancy and unit counts per property.
- **Recent Activity** — a record of recent payment, property, and other operational events.

Use **Add Tenant** for a quick tenant entry or **Collect Rent** to go directly to the collection queue.

## Work the collection queue

1. Open **Billing** from the sidebar.
2. Choose a queue filter: **All**, **Overdue**, **Due Today**, **Upcoming**, **Pending Review**, **Partial**, or **Paid**.
3. Open the lease or invoice that needs attention.
4. Review the invoice balance and payment attempts.
5. Record a manual payment, verify submitted proof, or recheck a pending gateway attempt.

The queue shows both the number of invoices and the total outstanding balance. Use the queue as a work list; use the invoice detail page as the source of truth for one tenant's balance.

![OpenKOS collection queue showing billing filters, progress, and unpaid invoices](/images/openkos-collection.jpg)

## Understand the statuses

- **Pending** — no confirmed payment has been allocated.
- **Partial** — at least one confirmed payment exists, but money is still outstanding.
- **Paid** — the invoice balance is fully settled by confirmed payments.
- **Overdue** — the invoice is still payable after its due date.
- **Pending review** — a payment or proof needs staff attention.

<Note>
  Dashboard totals are data-dependent. A count of zero means there is no matching work in the current installation, not that the feature is disabled.
</Note>
