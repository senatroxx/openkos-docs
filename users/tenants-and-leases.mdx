## Manage a tenant

Open **Tenants** to search tenant records, review their status, and open their workspace. Select **New Tenant** to add a tenant.

The tenant list makes portal access visible:

- **App access** — the tenant has an account for the portal.
- **Email only** — an email contact exists, but portal access is not active.
- **None** — no usable portal contact is connected.

From a tenant workspace, staff can upload documents, review leases, assign a unit, invite or resend an invitation, and disable portal access.

## Create a lease

Create a lease from the tenant or unit workspace. Confirm:

- Property and unit.
- Tenant or occupants.
- Start and end dates.
- Rent amount and billing interval.
- Billing strategy: advance or arrears.

When an active lease is created, OpenKOS generates the related invoices. Review the first invoice immediately so the due date and amount are clear before inviting the tenant.

## Lease workspace

The lease workspace brings related work together:

- Lease details and occupants.
- Documents.
- Invoices and invoice detail.
- Payments and payment proofs.
- Rent schedule.

## Change the lease

Common lifecycle actions are:

- **Renew** the lease when the current agreement continues.
- **Move** a tenant to another unit when the physical unit changes.
- **Move out** when the tenancy ends.
- **Send reminder** when staff need to contact the primary tenant immediately.

The lease is the agreement between the tenant and the property. Moving the tenant between units does not erase the agreement or its financial history.

<Note>
  Access to lease actions depends on role permissions. An admin may also be limited to properties assigned to them.
</Note>
