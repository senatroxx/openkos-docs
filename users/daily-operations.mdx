OpenKOS is organized around the work your team does every day. Use the page that matches the task in front of you.

## Properties and units

Use **Properties** to manage the places you operate. Within a property, you can review units, leases, documents, maintenance history, and lease history.

A unit can be:

- **Available** — ready for a new lease.
- **Occupied** — currently assigned to an active lease.
- **Maintenance** — blocked from new leases, assignments, and moves.
- **Unavailable** — blocked for another operational reason.

## Tenants and leases

Use **Tenants** to keep tenant records and documents together. From a tenant workspace, staff can review leases, assign a unit, send or resend an invitation, and disable portal access.

A lease connects a tenant to a property and unit. Common lease actions include:

- Creating and updating lease terms.
- Moving a tenant to another unit.
- Renewing a lease.
- Moving a tenant out.
- Reviewing invoices, payments, documents, and the rent schedule.

When a unit is blocked for maintenance, OpenKOS can either keep the tenant in place or transfer the active lease to another available unit. The lease remains the same; the unit transfer is tracked in history.

## Invoices and payments

OpenKOS uses the billing chain **lease → invoice → payment**.

- Pending invoices are generated for active leases.
- Partial payments are supported.
- Only confirmed payments change the invoice balance.
- Invoice status moves through pending, partial, and paid states.
- Overpayments are rejected.
- Staff can review payment proof and verify or reject submitted payments.

Use the invoice detail page as the source of truth for what is due and what has been paid.

## Maintenance

Create a maintenance ticket for a unit, describe the issue, and move it through **reported → in progress → resolved**.

When creating a ticket, you can optionally block the unit. Resolving a ticket can restore availability when no other open maintenance ticket still requires the unit to remain blocked.

## Rent reminders

OpenKOS can send configurable rent reminders through the installed notification channels. Automated reminders cover upcoming, due-today, and overdue invoices. Staff can also send a one-off reminder from a lease.

Reminder amounts come from the invoice's outstanding balance, so partial payments are reflected correctly.

<Note>
  What you can see and change depends on your role and permissions. If an action is missing, ask an owner or administrator to review your access.
</Note>
