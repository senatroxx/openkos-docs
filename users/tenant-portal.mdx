The tenant portal is the simpler, mobile-friendly side of OpenKOS. Tenants use it to answer the questions they need answered most often: What do I owe? What have I paid? What is the status of my request?

## First access

1. Open the invitation link sent by the property team.
2. Create or confirm your account credentials.
3. Verify your email if the installation requires it.
4. Sign in and open the portal dashboard.

If the invitation has expired or the email address is incorrect, ask the property team to resend it.

## Dashboard

The dashboard provides a quick view of the tenant's current lease, outstanding invoices, recent payments, notifications, and open maintenance tickets.

## Lease

Open **Lease** to review the active lease and its key details, including the property, unit, dates, rent, and billing information. If a tenant has more than one lease, select the lease they want to inspect.

## Payments and invoices

From **Billing**, tenants can:

- View current and past invoices.
- Open an invoice to see the amount, due date, and status.
- Pay through the configured gateway when online payments are enabled.
- Submit payment information or proof when the installation uses manual verification.
- Review payment history.
- Print or download invoice documents.

A payment may remain pending until the property team or payment provider confirms it. Do not submit the same payment again while the first attempt is still being checked.

## Maintenance requests

Open **Maintenance** to create a ticket, describe the problem, and follow its status. Add enough detail for the property team to understand the issue, including where it is and what changed.

## Notifications and profile

The portal keeps rent reminders and other account notifications in one place. Tenants can mark notifications as read and update their profile details from account settings.

<Tip>
  If a payment or maintenance request looks incorrect, contact the property team and include the invoice or ticket details shown in the portal.
</Tip>
