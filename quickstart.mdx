---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Go from an empty workspace to your first active lease."
---

Use this guide after your OpenKOS installation is available and you can sign in as an owner, admin, or staff user.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a property">
    Open **Properties** and add the property you manage. Add the address and any details your team needs when identifying it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add units">
    Open the property, add its units, and set each unit's type, rate, capacity, and availability. Units marked for maintenance should not be used for new leases.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a tenant">
    Open **Tenants**, create the tenant record, and add the contact details needed for invitations and notifications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a lease">
    From the tenant or unit workspace, create a lease and select the unit, dates, rent, billing interval, and billing strategy. OpenKOS generates the related invoices for the active lease.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the first invoice">
    Open the lease's **Invoices** workspace. Confirm the due date and amount, then record a payment manually or let a configured payment gateway handle checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite the tenant">
    Send the tenant invitation from the tenant workspace. After the tenant accepts, they can use the portal to view the lease, invoices, payment history, notifications, and maintenance tickets.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to check next

- Configure payment gateway, mail, and WhatsApp settings if your installation uses them.
- Set rent reminder timing and templates under **Settings → Reminders**.
- Create staff users and assign only the permissions they need.
- Open the dashboard to review rent and operational items that need attention.

<Tip>
  Start with one property and one lease. Once the full flow feels right, add the rest of your portfolio.
</Tip>
